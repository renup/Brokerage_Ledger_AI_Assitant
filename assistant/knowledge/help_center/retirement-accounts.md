# Retirement accounts

## Roth IRA and traditional IRA
A Roth IRA is funded with money that has already been taxed. A traditional IRA is usually funded with money that may be tax-deductible. The tax treatment of what you take out differs between the two.

## Taking money out of an IRA
You can ask to withdraw cash from an IRA the same way as from other accounts, and the same account checks apply. Whether a withdrawal triggers taxes or penalties depends on your age, the account type and your own situation. We cannot give tax advice. Ask a tax professional before withdrawing from an IRA.

## IRA fee
Roth and traditional IRAs have a $25 annual fee.

## Restricted accounts
An account can be marked restricted. While it is restricted, withdrawals are blocked. Contact support to find out why and what is needed to lift the restriction.
