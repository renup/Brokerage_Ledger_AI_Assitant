# Transferring an account from another broker

## How long a transfer takes
A transfer in from another broker usually takes 5 to 7 business days once the other broker has the request. Business days are Monday to Friday. The status moves from requested to in progress to completed.

## Why a transfer can fail
A transfer fails with an error code. The most common ones are:
- 214, name mismatch: the name on the other broker's account does not match the name on your account here.
- 215, account number mismatch: the account number was not found or did not match.
- 220, account type not accepted: the other broker cannot send this type of account.
- 230, missing signature: the transfer form needs a signature that is missing or does not match.
- 240, assets cannot be moved: some holdings cannot be transferred as they are.

## Fixing a failed transfer
Fix the cause first, then start the transfer again. For a name mismatch, make the name match at both brokers. For a missing signature, sign the form again. Resending a transfer needs your approval in the app, and it only works while the transfer is still failed.

## Moving an account out
Sending an account to another broker costs $75 per outgoing transfer.
