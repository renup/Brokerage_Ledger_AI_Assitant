# Moving money to your bank

## How long a withdrawal takes
A withdrawal to your linked bank takes 3 business days. Business days are Monday to Friday. Market holidays are not counted, so a holiday can add one day. While it is in progress it shows as pending, and the app shows which day of the 3 it is on.

## How much you can withdraw
You can withdraw the cash in your account, minus any withdrawals that are still pending. Money that is already promised to a pending withdrawal cannot be withdrawn twice. A single withdrawal can be at most $25,000. For a larger amount, make more than one withdrawal or contact support.

## When a withdrawal is blocked
A withdrawal is blocked if the account is restricted, if the amount is more than the cash available after pending withdrawals, or if it is over the $25,000 limit. The eligibility check in the app tells you which reason applies.

## Cancelling a withdrawal
You can ask to cancel a withdrawal while it is still pending. Once it has completed it cannot be cancelled. A cancellation always needs your approval in the app before anything changes.

## Withdrawal fees
An outgoing wire withdrawal costs $25. See the fees list in the app for the fees on your own accounts.
