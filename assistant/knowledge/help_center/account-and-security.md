# Your account and security

## Changing your legal name
Your legal name must match the name at other brokers and on your tax forms. A name change is an identity change, so it needs your approval in the app.

## Who can see your accounts
You can only see and ask about your own accounts. We cannot discuss another person's accounts, even a family member's.

## Talking to a person
You can ask for a person at any time. A support specialist can help with disputes, complaints, closing an account and anything the assistant cannot do. The assistant gives you a reference number to quote.

## Changes need your approval
The assistant can prepare a change, such as resending a transfer, cancelling a pending withdrawal or updating your name. It never makes the change itself. Nothing happens until you review and approve it in the app.
