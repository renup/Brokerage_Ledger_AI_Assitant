# Fees and charges

## What we charge
- Stock and ETF trades: $0.
- Outgoing account transfer to another broker: $75 each.
- Outgoing wire withdrawal: $25 each.
- Paper statements: $2 per month.
- IRA annual fee: $25 per year, for Roth and traditional IRAs only.

## Which fees apply to you
Fees depend on your account type. Individual accounts do not pay the IRA annual fee. The fees list in the app shows exactly the fees on your own accounts.
